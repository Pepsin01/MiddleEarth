## Decisions and facts worth remembering

- **Pictures.** The LOTR wiki's image host refuses automated downloads (403) and forbids embedding on other sites. Do not work around either. Characters use Wikimedia Commons photos of the actors (500px thumbnails, credited); events/places show a map excerpt and carry `suggestedPicture`, a link to a still on the wiki that the user can add by hand. Wikimedia rate-limits full-size downloads: use its standard thumbnail sizes and go slowly.
- **Wiki text and links** come from the wiki's public API, called from the browser with `origin=*` (`src/lib/wiki.ts`). That is the documented route for web pages.
- **Spoilers.** Season 3 started on 2026-11-11 and the user is watching it. Keep Season 3 episode titles, plot and promotional material out of the data and out of messages. On the wiki, "Halbrand" and "The Stranger" redirect to the spoiler pages, so those characters only get wiki links from their reveal events on.
- **Map.** "Middle Earth and Numenor – Tolkien Style Map" by KirsiKura (DeviantArt), 12098 × 9630 px. Númenor is an inset, and some labels are Third Age names (Arthedain, Cardolan) — not a strict Second Age map. Positions in the data are pixels of this picture; several places are marked "approximate" in their descriptions.
- **Routes.** An event's `via` points are followed only by characters who were on the map, were somewhere else, and for whom the first point is no farther away than the destination (`routeFor` in `shared/timeline.ts`).
- **The map is built once per map picture** (`MapView` is keyed by the map in `App.tsx`). Rebuilding it on data reload made layers run against a removed map.
- **Tests on Windows.** Use `npm test` (it wraps vitest); calling vitest directly fails in terminals that spell the drive letter in lowercase.
- **Content generator.** The story data was generated by a throwaway script that is not in the repo; the JSON files in `data/sagas/rings-of-power` are the source of truth.
